As a software producer, you are at the beginning of the SBOM lifecycle. Your primary responsibility is to create an accurate and comprehensive inventory of your software and share it with your customers and downstream stakeholders.

This section will guide you through the key activities of an SBOM producer, from generating your first SBOM to sharing it with the world.
