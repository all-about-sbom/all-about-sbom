An SBOM is more than a file that is created once. It becomes useful when it moves through three simple steps: someone creates it, makes it available, and uses the information it contains.

These steps are called **Produce**, **Distribute**, and **Consume**. They may be carried out by people, teams, automated tools, or a combination of all three.

Check out the pages below to learn more about the three steps of using an SBOM and the roles involved in each step.
