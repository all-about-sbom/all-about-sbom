## Challenges of Modern Software
Modern applications are built using third-party components, creating complex software supply chains. 
While this approach has positive effects, it also raises new challenges, for example in the areas of compliance and cybersecurity.
Here, the main challenge often lies not in solving the actual problem, but first in identifying which dependencies are used directly or indirectly within a software.

To create the needed transparency, the tech industry stared to adopt software bills of materials (SBOM).
Just as Bill of Materials (BOM) known from traditional manufacturing, SBOMs aim to list components within a software project in a standardized and machine-readable manner, enabling automated analysis.
This website provides information, best practices, and guidelines on SBOMs to further increase their adoption.

## SBOM Basics
The term "SBOM" refers primarily to a concept brought to life by the two major standards, CycloneDX and SPDX.
SBOMs in these formats can be generated at various stages of the SDLC.
The appropriate type of SBOM may vary depending on the use case. It is therefore important to briefly familiarize yourself with the technical basics.
