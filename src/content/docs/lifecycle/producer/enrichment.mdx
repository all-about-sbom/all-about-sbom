An SBOM is more than just a list of components. It can be enriched with a wide range of additional data to provide deeper insights into your software supply chain. This process is known as SBOM enrichment.

## Why Enrich Your SBOM?

Enriching your SBOM can help you to:

- **Improve security:** By adding vulnerability data to your SBOM, you can quickly identify and remediate security risks.
- **Ensure compliance:** By adding license data, you can ensure that you are complying with all your legal obligations.
- **Increase transparency:** By adding dependency relationship data, you can provide a more complete picture of your software supply chain.

## VEX: Vulnerability Exploitability eXchange

A key technology for enriching SBOMs with vulnerability information is the Vulnerability Exploitability eXchange (VEX). VEX is a companion to an SBOM that provides information about the exploitability of vulnerabilities in the context of the software product.

With VEX, a producer can state that a vulnerability is:
- **Not affected:** The vulnerability does not affect the product.
- **Affected:** The vulnerability affects the product and a patch is available.
- **Fixed:** The vulnerability has been fixed in the product.
- **Under investigation:** The producer is still investigating the vulnerability.

This allows consumers to focus on the vulnerabilities that actually matter, reducing noise and alert fatigue.
