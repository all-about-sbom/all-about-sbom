As a software producer, you are responsible for generating and providing SBOMs for your products. This section provides guidance on how to create, enrich, and share SBOMs.

## Key Activities

### SBOM Generation
Learn how to generate SBOMs for your software projects. This includes choosing the right tools and formats.

### SBOM Enrichment
Discover how to add valuable metadata to your SBOMs, such as vulnerability information and license data.

### SBOM Sharing
Understand the best practices for sharing your SBOMs with consumers and downstream stakeholders.
